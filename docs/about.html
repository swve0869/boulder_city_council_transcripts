About these summaries

This is an independent project, not an official city record.

Where the text comes from

City councils publish video of their meetings but rarely a written transcript. This site watches each city's YouTube channel for council meetings and saves the video's English captions. When the city uploads its own captions those are used; otherwise the captions are YouTube's automatic ones, which often get names, numbers and technical terms wrong.

How summaries are written

An AI model reads the full caption transcript of a meeting and writes: a short overview, a list of topics, a summary of each topic (five sentences at most), the timestamps where each topic begins and ends, and tags for each topic. The model's reply must pass an automatic structure check before it is published; the name of the model is shown on each meeting page.

Summaries can be wrong. The model can misread a garbled caption, attribute a remark to the wrong person, or miss something. Before quoting or relying on anything here, open the timestamp and watch that part of the meeting. Every topic links to its moment in the video, and "Read the transcript" shows the captions for just that topic.

Tags

Tags come from a fixed list of about 40 subjects (police, zoning, budget, wildfire and so on), grouped into eight areas that share a color. The model may only choose tags from that list. A few precise terms, such as "Flock" for license-plate-reader cameras, are also tagged automatically whenever they appear in a topic's title or summary.

Time estimates

"Time on a topic" is the span from the first to the last timestamp the model gave for it. When a topic has several tags, its time is divided among them with the first (most specific) tag weighted highest, so the times for one meeting add up to roughly its length. Topics that overlap, such as public comment that jumps between subjects, make these estimates approximate. Treat the charts on the Trends and Issues pages as a guide to where attention went, not a stopwatch.

Updates

New meetings are checked for several times an hour. A meeting appears under "Coming soon" while its captions are not yet available, which can take hours or a few days after a live stream. You can follow new summaries with the Atom feed in any feed reader.

Corrections and source code

The code that produces this site is public: source on GitHub. If you find an error in a summary, please open an issue there with a link to the meeting.
